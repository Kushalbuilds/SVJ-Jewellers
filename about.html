WHO WE ARE

Crafting Timeless Heirlooms in Solapur

At SVJ Jewellers, jewellery is more than adornment—it is an expression of devotion, milestone celebrations, and generational heritage. Situated at 478 Purv Mangalwar Peth, Baba Complex, Saraf Bazar, Solapur, our showroom brings together centuries-old Indian goldsmithing traditions and contemporary luxury design.

From intricate Maharashtrian bridal necklaces, temple nakshi bangles, and handcrafted kadas to modern 18K diamond solitaires and sacred 999 pure silver pooja articles, every piece undergoes rigorous quality inspection. We believe true luxury rests on complete transparency—every gold ornament is BIS Hallmarked with HUID verification, and our daily bullion rates are benchmarked directly to All India Bullion (AIB) standards.

Whether you are curating a wedding trousseau, investing in 24K pure gold, or commissioning a bespoke family design, our specialists offer personalized guidance, honest weight-to-purity documentation, and lifetime after-sales care.
